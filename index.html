Design Parameters

Engineered around functional interface ergonomics.

[GAUGES]

Digital Tachometer Accent

Operators utilize high-contrast warm ember (`#ff9e64`) and active sage green (`#9ece6a`) highlights. Immediate readability with low cognitive fatigue.

[LED_TRIM]

Electric Ambient Guides

A custom electric lavender accent line (`#bb9af7`) is embedded for active tabs, cursor selections, and code brackets. Intuitive active state layout.

[CABIN]

Anti-Glare Surfaces

Available in Carbon Slate or absolute Carbon OLED. Includes Silver Metallic and Sunset Ivory daytime cockpit skins to suit high exterior light levels.

Cockpit Presets

Optimized styles for both nighttime highway cruises and high-brilliance daytime coding.

Obsidian Glow (Default) Carbon Slate
Signature deep carbon slate with digital indicators and electric lavender highlights.
Obsidian Glow - Carbon OLED Pitch Black
Unlit space cockpit dashboard. Extreme active contrast ratios.
Obsidian Glow - Ambient Light Daylight
Metallic silver day panel. High instrument density day styling.
Obsidian Glow - Sunset Sand Sunset Leather
Beige ivory cockpit with sunset espresso indicators.
